Logic behind CoinFlip and the nature of Blockchain:
Blockchains require deterministic execution for consensus, which means no on-chain data source can ever be truly unpredictable — using block.* values for anything security-critical (randomness, timing-sensitive logic) is always exploitable by anyone able to read or compute that same public data. The fix is always randomness generated and verified off-chain (VRF) or split across transactions (commit-reveal) — never an on-chain trick.
